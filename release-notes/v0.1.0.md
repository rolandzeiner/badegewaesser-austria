## Summary
First release. Water quality and temperature for all 260 EU-designated Austrian bathing waters, from the official AGES dataset — no API key, no YAML.

## Breaking changes
_None._

## Added
- One config entry per bathing water, chosen either by Bundesland or by distance from your Home Assistant location.
- Six sensors per bathing water: water temperature, E. coli, enterococci, Secchi depth, the annual EU classification, and the date of the newest sample.
- Two binary sensors: `Gesperrt` (device class `problem`, with the authority's stated reason) and `Badesaison`.
- A Lovelace card built around the season rather than the moment. The samples sit on a calendar axis from 15 May to 30 September, so the ~20-day sampling rhythm and the age of the newest reading are both visible at a glance.
- Detection limits are shown as limits. Most samples report "below the laboratory's detection limit" rather than a measured count, so the card renders `<15` and the sensor carries a `below_detection_limit` attribute — reading a limit as a measurement would overstate contamination at a clean site.
- Entities stay available out of season. From October to mid-May nothing new is sampled, but last summer's readings and the annual classification are still valid and still shown.
- One HTTP request per poll regardless of how many bathing waters you follow — all 260 arrive in a single document.
- Diagnostics, a repair issue for a bathing water that disappears upstream, and German and English throughout.

## Known limitations
- Samples are 4 to 9 per season, about 20 days apart. This is not live data, and every reading is shown with the date it was taken.
- One bathing water (*Wolfgangsee, St. Gilgen – Gamsjaga*) has no coordinates upstream, so it cannot appear in the "near you" list. It can still be added by Bundesland.
- Closures have not been observed in live data — `TGESPERRT` was `0` for all 260 sites while this was built — so the closure banner follows the documented shape rather than an observed one.

## Compatibility
- Home Assistant: ≥ 2025.6.0
- HACS: ≥ 2.0.0
