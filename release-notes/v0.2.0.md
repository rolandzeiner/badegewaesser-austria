## Summary
A readings grid with trend arrows, a map marker for every bathing water, and no polling from October to mid-May, when nothing in the data can change.

## Breaking changes
_None._

## Added
- Each bathing water appears as a marker on Home Assistant's map. Only the water-temperature sensor carries the position, so each lake gets one marker, not eight.
- A pin after the bathing water's name on the card opens it on OpenStreetMap. Nothing loads from OpenStreetMap until you select it.
- The readings sit in a two-by-two grid, each with its own icon. Water quality shows the EU classification symbol beside the class, from ★★★ for excellent to – for poor.
- Secchi depth, E. coli and enterococci show an arrow for how they moved since the previous sample. Changes too small to matter read as level.
- Every sample's temperature is printed above its dot on the season track. Hover or tap the Secchi depth, E. coli or enterococci tile to show that reading's season on the track instead.
- A new card option, `show_attribution`, hides the data-source line. The entities carry the attribution either way.
- The integration's own icon, which Home Assistant 2026.3 and newer shows in Settings.

## Changed
- The integration no longer polls from 1 October to 14 May. The next poll comes on 15 May, and through September it checks once a day for late lab results. A failing poll keeps retrying, so an outage in autumn can't leave the entities unavailable until May.
- The off-season interval option is gone from the entry's **Configure** dialog. A stored value is ignored.
- The card's header puts the water temperature beside the bathing water's name and the sample date beside its Bundesland, which makes the card two rows shorter.
- The card editor picks a bathing water by its device instead of by one of its eight entities. Cards set up with `entity` keep working.
- The card's season axis ends on 31 August, the last day samples are taken, and the "Badesaison läuft" line under it is gone. The `Badesaison` entity still reports the season for automations.
- The attribution line reads "Datenquelle: AGES · CC BY 3.0 AT", so it fits on one line.

## Fixed
- `Badesaison` follows Austria's bathing season, 15 June to 31 August (Badegewässerverordnung § 4). It used Germany's, 15 May to 30 September, and so read "on" through September.
- *Wolfgangsee, St. Gilgen – Gamsjaga* now has a position, taken from its bathing-water profile, so it appears in the "near you" list.
- A restart fetched the AGES document once for every bathing water you follow. It now fetches it once in total.
- The card fills its grid cell instead of painting over the section below it.
- A new year's classification is read from the document. Before, the integration would have kept reporting 2026's class as current.

## Compatibility
- Home Assistant: ≥ 2025.6.0
- HACS: ≥ 2.0.0
